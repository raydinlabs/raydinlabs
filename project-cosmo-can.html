COSMO CAN

From onboard avionics to the ground station

Pramath developed the CanSat avionics and firmware, together with the ground station control hardware and software. The project connects the onboard system with its ground-side interface.

Engineering scope

  • CanSat avionics hardware
  • Onboard embedded firmware
  • Ground station control hardware
  • Ground station software

Development & validation

The work spans the onboard electronics, embedded firmware, and ground-side controls. These are the connected parts of the system, from sensing onboard to interaction at the ground station.

Detailed build records, test results, and demonstration footage have not yet been published.
