What is
Raydin Labs?

An independent engineering and research initiative founded by Pramath Bhat, working at the intersection of electronics, embedded software, and experimental aerospace systems.

Our work connects the details of a circuit board to the behavior of an entire system. From CanSat avionics and ground stations to modular flight controllers, we build with a focus on understanding, iteration, and practical use.

Alongside our own projects, we offer commercial engineering services for people and teams developing hardware products.

Where we
want to go.

Build an enduring technology company that turns sustained research into useful, accessible products.

Our long-term ambition is to grow from focused engineering projects into proprietary hardware platforms, reusable technologies, and dedicated research capabilities. This is a direction we are working toward—not a claim of current scale.
