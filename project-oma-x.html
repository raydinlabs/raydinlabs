Designed to evolve with the experiment

OMA – X is an upcoming modular flight controller architecture focused on research, repairability, and experimentation.

Design direction

  • Replaceable modules
  • Repairability as a design goal
  • Flexible experimentation
  • Reusable interfaces

Development & validation

The architecture aims to let individual modules evolve as experiment requirements change. It remains in development; final hardware, benchmarks, and demonstrations are not yet published.
